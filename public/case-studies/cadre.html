Cadre Agent Team Framework

Cadre is a spec-driven framework for defining AI-human software teams: agent roles, skills, schemas, workflow modes, templates, validation, and evaluation procedures for reliable agentic development.

YAMLAgent, team, skill, and workflow configuration as structured specs.
ValidationSchema checks, tests, and hooks to keep agent definitions consistent.
EvaluationRubrics and cold-start procedures for measuring team behavior.

What I Built

I designed the system around composable agent definitions, reusable skill specs, team templates, and adapters for different orchestration frameworks. The goal is to make AI development teams inspectable, testable, and easier to evolve than a pile of one-off prompts.

Architecture
