Cadre Agent Team Framework

Cadre is a framework concept for organizing AI assistants into role-based teams that collaborate, review one another’s work, and keep a human operator in control of important decisions.

Role-basedDistinct perspectives for architecture, implementation, review, and quality.
CollaborativeStructured review loops help agents challenge and improve one another’s work.
Human-ledOperators review meaningful work products and retain final decision authority.

What I Built

I designed the concept around reusable roles, workflows, review checkpoints, and clear attribution. The goal is to make AI-assisted development more inspectable and dependable than a single assistant working without structured review.

Engineering Focus

Collaboration

  • Role-based perspectives with distinct responsibilities
  • Reusable workflows for drafting, review, revision, and handoff
  • Multi-perspective feedback instead of isolated agent output
  • Clear attribution so operators can understand where feedback came from

Trust & Control

  • Quality checkpoints before work reaches the operator
  • Visible review context and unresolved decisions
  • Persistent context designed around operator control
  • Provider-flexible architecture as a long-term design goal
