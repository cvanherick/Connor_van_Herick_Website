Happen Bank Collections Strategy

Built data and machine-learning decision systems to help a collections strategy team evaluate borrower treatment choices at scale.

Presenting project results at the end of the internship. This page is a high-level portfolio summary and omits confidential implementation details.

Scale73M+ records processed
StackPySpark, SQL, Databricks
ModelingXGBoost uplift modeling
Result2.04% charge-off reduction
Value$20.4M estimated gross value

Problem

Collections strategy requires making high-volume decisions under operational, compliance, and capacity constraints. The project focused on turning large-scale borrower and account data into a more measurable decision process.

Approach

Data architecture

Built production-oriented PySpark and SQL pipelines in Databricks, with feature construction and evaluation designed for large datasets.

Decision modeling

Applied XGBoost S- and T-Learner approaches to estimate treatment effects, then evaluated results on holdout data with business constraints in mind.

Result

The holdout evaluation showed a 2.04% charge-off reduction and an estimated $20.4M in gross value. The public summary intentionally excludes internal datasets, feature names, decision rules, and proprietary business context.
